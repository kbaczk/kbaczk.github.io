Selected work

Adjustable H-Beam Spreader — SMR Lifting System

Group project · 2025 · Client brief: Rolls-Royce SMR Ltd

Contributed a weight-optimised design concept to a 5-person team developing an adjustable H-beam spreader for lifting reactor modules, selected via Pugh Matrix evaluation. Carried out beam-section selection analysis across the full Universal Beam catalogue (bending stress and deflection under a 20-tonne load) to identify the lightest section meeting LOLER 1998 and BS EN 13001 requirements. Produced SolidWorks assemblies and BS 8888-compliant technical drawings.

Gesture-Controlled Robotic Arm

Independent project · 2026 — ongoing

Designing and building an Arduino-controlled robotic arm that mimics hand gestures captured by a flex-sensor and MPU6050 IMU glove over Bluetooth. Covers circuit design, servo control programming, and CAD design of custom laser-cut components — including adapting a mantis-style gripper mechanism with a spring-loaded gripper tip.
